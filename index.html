Research

I work on robot learning: teaching robots from human video, cross-embodiment policies, and understanding what vision-language-action models represent. (* equal contribution)

Progress Probes (P²): Lightweight Runtime Monitoring for Vision-Language-Action Models

Atiksh Bhardwaj, Edward W. Duan, Junwon Seo, Prithwish Dan, Wei-Chiu Ma, Andrea Bajcsy, Preston Culbertson

arXiv preprint, 2026

project page arXiv

Linear probes on π0.5's activations, trained only on successful demos, estimate task progress and flag failing rollouts at a fraction of the cost of VLM-based monitors.

X-Diffusion: Training Diffusion Policies on Cross-Embodiment Human Demonstrations

Maximus A. Pace*, Prithwish Dan*, Chuanruo Ning, Atiksh Bhardwaj, Audrey Du, Edward W. Duan, Wei-Chiu Ma, Kushal Kedia

ICRA 2026

project page arXiv code data

Human demonstrations are noised until they are indistinguishable from robot actions, letting diffusion policies learn from human video without copying infeasible motions.

X-Sim: Cross-Embodiment Learning via Real-to-Sim-to-Real

Prithwish Dan*, Kushal Kedia*, Angela Chao, Edward W. Duan, Maximus A. Pace, Wei-Chiu Ma, Sanjiban Choudhury

CoRL 2025 Oral

project page arXiv code

Robot policies learned from human RGBD videos alone, by rebuilding the scene in simulation, training with RL on object motion, and transferring back to the real world.
